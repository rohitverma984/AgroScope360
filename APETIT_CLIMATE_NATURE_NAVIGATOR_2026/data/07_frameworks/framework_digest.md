# Framework digest (status 3 November 2026)

Short, original summaries of the rules this challenge touches, written for builders.
They are not substitutes for the source documents: download those from the links in
`source_registry.csv` and cite them in your solution.  Several frameworks changed in 2026;
the dates below matter for anything Apetit decides in 2027.

## Climate targets: SBTi

* **Apetit's status.** Committed in December 2025 to set near-term targets (24 months to submit).
* **Corporate Net-Zero Standard.** V2.0 published 11 June 2026, effective 1 February 2027.
  V1.3.1 remains usable until end-2027; V2 is mandatory for new and renewed targets from
  31 January 2028.  V2 prefers the most recent year with comprehensive data as base year and
  five-year near-term targets.
* **Minimum ambition (V1.3.1 method appendix).** Scope 1+2: 4.2 %/yr linear (1.5 C).
  Scope 3: 2.5 %/yr (well-below 2 C).  FLAG: 3.03 %/yr.  Long-term (net-zero): at least -90 %
  for Scope 1+2 and for Scope 3 by 2050; FLAG -72 % by 2050.  Near-term targets cover 5-10 years
  from submission (V2: 5 years).
* **FLAG.** Mandatory for food production and food & beverage processing companies, and for
  any company whose FLAG emissions exceed 20 % of the total.  Requires a commitment to eliminate
  deforestation, with a cut-off date no later than 2020 and a target date for full compliance
  (originally end-2025 in v1.0; check v1.2 for current phase-in rules).  FLAG = land-based emissions and removals:
  on-farm N2O and CH4, drained organic soils, liming, land-use change, soil carbon.  Fertiliser
  manufacturing, farm diesel and processing are non-FLAG.

## Accounting: GHG Protocol

* **Corporate Standard / Scope 3 Standard.** Base-year recalculation after structural changes
  (acquisitions, divestments) and methodology changes above a significance threshold.
* **Scope 2.** Report both location-based (grid average) and market-based (contractual
  instruments such as guarantees of origin, else residual mix).  A revised Scope 2 / Scope 3
  consultation is planned for 2027.
* **Land Sector and Removals Standard (LSR).** v1.0 January 2026, v1.1 June 2026, effective
  1 January 2027.  Covers land management emissions, land-use change, removals (optional to
  report; must be monitored, traceable and reversals accounted for) and biogenic products.

## Nature: SBTN, TNFD, ESRS

* **SBTN.** Steps: 1 assess (1a materiality screening on ENCORE pressures, 1b value-chain
  assessment), 2 interpret & prioritise (pressure x state of nature), 3 measure, set and
  disclose targets (freshwater quantity and quality, land), 4 act, 5 track.  SBTN 2.0 was
  launched in October 2026 (adds groundwater and pesticides; supplier-engagement targets);
  V1 methods valid for validation until April 2027.
* **Freshwater quality.** Nutrient targets focus on the limiting nutrient (often phosphorus in
  Finnish lakes) at sub-basin level; state-of-nature water layers combine Aqueduct and nutrient
  models.
* **TNFD LEAP 2.0** (October 2026): Locate - Evaluate - Assess - Prepare.
* **ESRS (revised, 2026).**  E1 climate (transition plan, scenario analysis, targets, energy,
  gross Scope 1/2/3, biogenic CO2 separately, removals), E3 water (consumption in areas of water
  stress = Aqueduct baseline water stress "high" or above, or WEI+ >= 40 %), E4 biodiversity.
  After the 2026 Omnibus, CSRD scope is > 1,000 employees and > EUR 450 m turnover, so Apetit
  is expected to report voluntarily (e.g. VSME/GRI) rather than under mandatory ESRS.

## Regulation affecting the value chain

* **EU Deforestation Regulation.** Applies from 30 December 2026 (large and medium operators).
  Relevant commodities: cattle, cocoa, coffee, palm oil, rubber, soya, wood - but scope is set
  product by product by the HS codes in Annex I (e.g. soya: 1201 beans, 1507 oil, 2304 meal).
  Soy protein isolate (HS 3504) is not listed, and rapeseed is not a relevant commodity.  Annex I
  can be amended by delegated act, so screen HS codes, not commodity names.
* **EU ETS2.** Carbon pricing of fuels for buildings, road transport and small industry; start
  postponed to 2028.  Relevant for LNG/LPG/diesel cost pathways.
* **CBAM.** Definitive phase from 2026 covers fertilisers -> input-cost risk for growers.

## Scenarios

* **NGFS Phase V** (November 2024; usability notes November 2025): Net Zero 2050, Below 2 C,
  Low Demand, Delayed Transition, Fragmented World, NDCs, Current Policies.  This dataset ships
  illustrative carbon-price paths in the NGFS ranges; pull official values from the IIASA NGFS
  explorer (variable Price|Carbon) for production use.
* **Physical climate (Finland).** FMI CMIP6 projections (Ruosteenoja & Jylhä 2021): by
  2040-2069 vs 1981-2010, Finland warms +2.3 / +2.7 / +3.6 C (SSP1-2.6 / 2-4.5 / 5-8.5), summer
  +2.1 / +2.4 / +3.2 C; summer precipitation in the south changes little, so early-summer
  drought risk increases with higher evaporation; heavy-rain intensity increases.
